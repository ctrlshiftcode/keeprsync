# Privacy Policy — KeeprSync

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

KeeprSync ("KeeprSync", "we", "us", or "our") is a Google Chrome extension designed to synchronize browser bookmarks across the user's computers using Google Drive.

This Privacy Policy explains what information KeeprSync accesses, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

## 1. Information We Access

To provide its synchronization functionality, KeeprSync may access and process:

* Bookmarks stored in Google Chrome, including bookmark names, URLs, folders, subfolders, and bookmark order.
* Information required to identify and access the Google Drive account authorized by the user.
* Synchronization files created by KeeprSync in Google Drive.

KeeprSync uses this information solely to provide the bookmark synchronization functionality requested by the user.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by KeeprSync is used to:

* Synchronize bookmarks between the user's computers.
* Preserve bookmark folders and subfolders.
* Preserve bookmark order.
* Detect changes to bookmarks.
* Update synchronized data when manual or automatic synchronization is performed.
* Create, update, and read the files required for synchronization in Google Drive.

KeeprSync does not use bookmarks for advertising, profiling, or selling user information.

## 3. Google Drive

KeeprSync uses Google Drive as the storage and synchronization service.

The extension requests only the permissions necessary to perform its synchronization functions. Files used by KeeprSync remain associated with the user's own Google Drive account.

KeeprSync does not sell, rent, or commercially distribute user data.

## 4. Sharing of Information

KeeprSync does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be transmitted to Google through Google Drive APIs and services solely when necessary to perform the synchronization requested by the user.

## 5. Storage and Servers

KeeprSync does not maintain copies of users' bookmarks on its own servers, unless explicitly stated in a future version of the extension.

Data used for synchronization is stored in the user's Google Drive through Google's services.

## 6. Security

KeeprSync uses Google's APIs and authorization mechanisms to protect access to the data required for the extension to function.

Users are responsible for keeping their Google accounts and devices secure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and therefore absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. User Control

Users may stop synchronization or uninstall KeeprSync from Chrome at any time.

Users may also revoke the permissions granted to KeeprSync through their Google Account settings.

Uninstalling the extension does not necessarily delete synchronization files that have already been created in Google Drive. These files can be deleted by the user directly from Google Drive.

## 8. Children's Privacy

KeeprSync is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KeeprSync's functionality changes or when applicable legal requirements change.

When a material change is made, the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy will be updated accordingly.
